### 2. assistant — 2025-12-28 04:46:11Z

NDnet files are DisPerSE’s native binary “network” containers. They are not text; the header begins with `NDNETWORK` and the type (e.g., `delaunay_tesselation`, as in `outputs/snap_010_thinned/snap_010.NDnet`). Inside, DisPerSE stores the full simplicial complex and its attributes:

- Node table: coordinates of every sampled point (and DTFE density/critical information that DisPerSE uses).
- Connectivity: the Delaunay simplices (edges/triangles/tetrahedra) or, after `mse`, the extracted manifolds/skeleton cells corresponding to the chosen persistence cut.
- Per-element metadata: persistence labels, critical-point indices, orientation, bounding box, and other flags needed by `netconv`/`skelconv` to convert the network to VTU/VTP/PLY or to feed into later DisPerSE steps.

You generally don’t read NDnet directly; use `netconv` to export it (e.g., VTU) if you want to inspect the contents.
