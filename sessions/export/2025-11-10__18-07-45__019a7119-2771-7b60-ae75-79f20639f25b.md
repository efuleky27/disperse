what is Show() and Render()?

<a id="m-0052"></a>
### 52. assistant — 2025-11-11 06:28:26Z

In ParaView’s scripting API:

- `Show(source, view)` tells the active view to display a pipeline object (like your wall mesh). It creates or updates the “display properties” for that source—basically the same as clicking the eyeball icon in the GUI.

- `Render()` asks the current view to redraw. In the GUI, renders happen automatically when you change something, but in scripts you call `Render()` to force the window (or off-screen buffer) to update after changing pipeline/display settings.

So `Show(...)` adds the data to the scene, and `Render()` makes the updated scene visible.
